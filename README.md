# Wafra: Solar and Renewable Energy Analytics

> How much gas and money can solar and renewable energy save in electricity generation? We measure it with real data from a benchmark country.

---

## Project Name

**Wafra** (وفرة)

*Wafra means abundance in Arabic: the abundance of clean energy, and the savings it brings.*

---

## Project Idea

Wafra studies how solar and other renewable energy can reduce the natural gas burned to generate electricity, and how much money that avoids.

Reliable, detailed energy data is not equally available everywhere. So we choose a **benchmark country** with trustworthy data (selected in week 1) and measure what renewables actually achieved there: how fast their share grew, how output changes by season, how much demand they covered, and how much gas they displaced.

A small comparison section then asks what similar results could mean for Egypt, using clearly stated assumptions.

**Goal:** a transparent, data-backed picture of the gas and cost savings that renewable energy can deliver, with every assumption and limitation stated openly.

**Note:** avoided gas is an *estimate* (the gas-fired generation that renewable energy replaced), not a measured saving.

## Roles & Responsibilities

Each member owns **one complete mini-project (pipeline)** on one energy topic, from data collection to insights. The pipelines are connected: they share the same country, period, and table format, and several depend on each other's outputs.

**Topic pool (assigned at kickoff):** solar and renewable generation, conventional (gas) generation, electricity demand, costs and prices, savings and impact.

**Every member covers the same pipeline:**

1. Data collection and inventory
2. Data wrangling, with a log of every cleaning decision
3. Analytical questions for the topic
4. KPIs for the topic
5. Key findings
6. Visuals
7. Limitations
8. A short note applying the topic to Egypt

**Integration:** one member assembles the final small dashboard; the others deliver clean tables and their visuals.

---

## KPIs

We will build KPIs specific to the data, focused on how much gas and money renewable energy saves. These include, among others:

- **Monthly savings:** gas-fired generation replaced by solar and renewable energy, and the estimated money saved
- **Yearly savings:** the same savings, per year
- **Total savings** over the whole study period
- **Gas vs solar generation** compared month by month
- **Renewable share** of total electricity generation (%)
- **Year-over-year growth** in renewable generation
- **Best and weakest months** (seasonal pattern)

More KPIs will be added as the analysis develops.
